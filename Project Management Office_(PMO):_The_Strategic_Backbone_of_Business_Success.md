What is a Project Management Office (PMO)?
A Project Management Office (PMO) is a centralized department within an organization responsible for standardizing project execution, defining best practices, ensuring resource optimization, and aligning all projects with business strategy. It operates as a support system for project managers and executive leadership to ensure on-time, on-budget, and high-quality project delivery.

Why PMOs Matter More Than Ever
As businesses scale, so do the number and complexity of their initiatives. Without a unified system of project oversight, organizations risk delays, scope creep, misallocation of resources, and lack of alignment with broader goals.
